    Ask Daniel
    Do I need to show W-2s or tax returns?

    No. A DSCR loan qualifies on the property's rental income compared with its payment (PITIA), not on your personal income. Credit, reserves and down payment still matter, but your tax returns and write-offs don't work against you.

    What DSCR ratio do I need?

    It depends on the lender. Some programs want a 1.25 minimum, others work at 1.0, and a few will go below 1.0. With access to 250+ lenders, Daniel matches the deal to the program instead of forcing it into one box.

    Can I use Airbnb or VRBO income?

    Often, yes. Many programs now accept short-term rental income, and some will qualify using AirDNA market projections. That's exactly how one of Daniel's clients turned a long-term-rent 'no' into a closed cash-out refinance.

    What property types qualify?

    Single-family rentals, 2–4 unit multifamily (duplex, triplex, quad), short-term rentals, and most condos and townhomes. Condo warrantability rules can apply depending on the lender.

    Can I buy or refinance in my LLC?

    Many DSCR programs allow you to close in an LLC. Tell Daniel how you hold title today and he'll line up programs that fit your structure.

    How much do I need to put down?

    Down payment depends on the lender, your credit and the property. It's one of the biggest levers on your DSCR, which is why the calculator lets you test it. Daniel will show you the trade-offs side by side.

    Can I use a DSCR loan for a flip?

    Not usually. DSCR loans are built for rentals you plan to hold. For a fix-and-flip, a private or hard money loan is often the better tool, and Daniel can place those too.

    How fast can we close?

    The team averages a 17-day close. Every file is different, but fewer income documents means fewer hold-ups, and repeat deals move faster once you've been through it once.
